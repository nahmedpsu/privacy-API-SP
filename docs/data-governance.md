# Authorization and data-governance boundary

Publishing this scaffold grants no permission to access a protected MIS or share its data. Written owner scope, institutional ethics determination and data-governance arrangements must precede operational evaluation.

## Required execution controls

- **Scope:** Approved environment, endpoint allowlist, operations, bounded records and negative-test procedures.
- **Identities:** Designated research accounts and operator-supervised transitions.
- **Traffic:** Rate/request ceilings, bounded retries, stop procedure and incident contact.
- **Evidence:** Owner-approved correlation, upstream visibility, ingestion windows and provenance classification.
- **Data:** In-environment processing, access restriction, retention/deletion schedule and reviewed exports.
- **Disclosure:** Coordinated notification and remediation; no public issue with sensitive findings.

## Public versus restricted

Public materials may include newly authored software, generalized schemas, invented configuration examples and approved aggregates. Do not publish institutional archives, internal specifications, credentials, live routes, beneficiary identifiers, raw responses or detailed logs simply because researchers can access them.

Use non-derivable aliases in approved public outputs. Hashes of predictable identifiers must not be treated as sufficient anonymization, and small aggregates or detailed findings may still be sensitive.

## Fixture policy

The example contract contains invented names and no beneficiary records. It exists solely to test parser and CLI behavior.

Development tests and fixtures are not evidence of operational privacy conformance. Primary evaluation must use the actual approved implementation and governed operational records/extract, with any limitation disclosed rather than silently substituted.
